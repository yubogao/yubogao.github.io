About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Artificial Intelligence Thrust, Information Hub at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), where I began my doctoral studies in January 2026.

My research interests include efficient reasoning and inference for large language models, LLM agents and multi-agent systems, and multimodal understanding. My work spans adaptive reasoning budgets, KV cache compression, instruction-following analysis, and multimodal sentiment and physics reasoning.

Previously, I was a research assistant at HKUST (Guangzhou) and a research intern at the Centre for Frontier AI Research, A*STAR. I received my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from North University of China in 2022.
