About Me

I am a PhD student in the Artificial Intelligence Thrust, Information Hub at HKUST (GZ), where I began my doctoral studies in January 2026. Previously, I was a research assistant at HKUST (GZ) and a research intern at A*STAR.

My research focuses on efficient LLM reasoning and inference, agent planning and scheduling, and multimodal understanding.
